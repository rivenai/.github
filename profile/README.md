Riven AI

Riven AI builds operator infrastructure for AI-native businesses — a unified platform spanning the gateway, workspace console, catalog, memory, ERP, and telephony surfaces that small teams need to run.

This organization hosts the source-of-truth repositories for the Riven AI platform. Most repositories are private; public surfaces live here.

Platform

riven-platform — monorepo for gateway, workspace console, catalog, and infrastructure
riven-memory — ingestion, entity resolution, enrichment, and retrieval
riven-erp-enterprise — ERP surface
riven-pbx — telephony and voice routing
